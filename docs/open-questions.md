# Open questions

Answered 2026-10-31 (see brief.md and decisions.md). Still open:

1. **Structure**: one carcass with 1–4 internal bays (proposed), or separately built cabinets side by side?
   Note: a large one-piece wardrobe may not fit through Egyptian apartment doors/lifts/stairs, so modular
   build + assembly on site is probably needed anyway. Ask the woodworkers how they build today.
2. **Size limits**: confirm the proposed defaults with the woodworkers.
3. **Materials list**: which woods/boards are actually available and common (e.g. beech زان, oak أرو,
   pine موسكي, MDF, plywood كونتر, melamine)? Rough cost per sheet for the pricing formula.
4. **Payment**: is there (or will there be) a registered Egyptian company? Gateways like Paymob need one.
   For the sales prototype, is a manual deposit (InstaPay / Vodafone Cash / bank transfer) acceptable?
5. **Saving designs**: is a shareable link enough for the prototype, or are accounts (phone number + OTP) needed?
6. **Lead capture**: should "order" in the prototype send the design to WhatsApp of the sales contact?
7. **Brand name** and domain.
8. Competitor review: look at Tylko, Mycs, IKEA PAX/PLATSA planner, raumplus, and any Egyptian custom
   wardrobe makers (دولاب تفصيل) on Instagram/Facebook, which is likely where local competition sells.
